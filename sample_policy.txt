ACME HEALTHTECH PVT. LTD.
ACCESS CONTROL POLICY
Version 1.0

1. Purpose
This policy defines how access to Acme HealthTech information systems is granted, used and removed, to protect company and customer data.

2. Scope
This policy applies to all employees, contractors and interns who access Acme HealthTech systems, including the patient portal, AWS cloud environment and internal applications.

3. User Accounts
3.1 Every user must be given a unique user ID. Shared accounts are not allowed.
3.2 Passwords must be at least 8 characters long.

4. Granting Access
4.1 Access to any system must be requested through the IT helpdesk ticketing system.
4.2 Access must be approved by the user's reporting manager before it is granted.
4.3 Users are given only the access required for their job role (least privilege). Role-based access groups are used wherever possible.

5. Privileged Access
5.1 Administrator access is limited to the IT team.
5.2 Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is required for administrator accounts.

6. Removing Access
6.1 When an employee leaves the company, HR informs IT and the user's access is removed.

7. Remote Access
7.1 Employees may access company systems from home using the company VPN.

8. Violations
Violation of this policy may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination.
